# Byte-exact expectations for the mBooster calibration flow, taken verbatim
# from the two 2026-09-08 Pit House captures ("Brake pedal travel calibration"
# and "Brake motor calibration", VID 346e PID 0008, single unit at dev 0x12).
#
#   tools/cmd-frame --check tools/cmd-frame-mbooster-cal.txt
#
# Format:  <expected hex> | <command-name> read|write [value] [device]
# Device defaults to 0x12.

# --- connect / post-reboot identity handshake, in Pit House's own order

7e 01 02 12 00 a0             | mbooster-device-presence read

7e 01 0f 12 01 ae             | mbooster-sw-version     read
7e 01 11 12 04 b3             | mbooster-identity-11    read
7e 01 08 12 01 a7             | mbooster-hw-version     read
7e 01 10 12 00 ae             | mbooster-serial-a       read
7e 01 08 12 02 a8             | mbooster-hw-sub         read
7e 01 10 12 01 af             | mbooster-serial-b       read

# --- plain virtual damping (0xAD): captured values 0x6666 press (40%,
#     firmware decoded 4.80000) and 0x4000 release (25%, 2.50003)

7e 05 24 12 ad 00 01 40 00 b4 | mbooster-brake-damping-release write 0x4000
